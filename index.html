📖 How to Play SOS

🎯 Goal: Make as many SOS combinations as possible.

1️⃣ Choose S or O.

2️⃣ Tap any empty box on the board.

3️⃣ Make S-O-S horizontally, vertically or diagonally.

4️⃣ Each SOS gives you 10 points.

5️⃣ Making SOS gives you another turn.

🏆 Player with the highest score wins!
